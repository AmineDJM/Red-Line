## Principle

Consumers normally have **14 days** to withdraw from a distance purchase, without giving any reason.

## Exception for digital content supplied immediately

Under Article **L221-28 13°** of the French Consumer Code, the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised for the
supply of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium whose performance has begun after the consumer's
**prior express consent** and **express waiver** of their right of withdrawal.

Red Line premium currency is credited to the account **immediately** after payment.

## Your consent

Before each purchase, a checkbox asks you to confirm that:

1. you expressly request immediate supply of the digital content, before the end of the withdrawal period;
2. you acknowledge that you **lose your right of withdrawal** upon delivery.

Without this confirmation, the purchase cannot be confirmed. A summary of this consent is kept with the order and
can be sent to you on request at {{legal.contactEmail}}.

## What remains guaranteed

Waiving the right of withdrawal does not remove the statutory guarantees: in the event of a billing error, double
payment, non-delivery or lack of conformity, see the [terms of sale](page:legal:cgv).
