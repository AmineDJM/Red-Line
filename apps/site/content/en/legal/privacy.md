**Payments** are processed by Stripe. Red Line receives and stores **no payment card data**: only the amount,
date, status and technical identifiers of the transaction are passed on.

No automated decision producing legal effects is taken about you. Anti-cheat tools flag situations to a human
moderator, who decides.

## 3. Recipients and processors

Your data is **never sold**. It is only accessible to the publisher, to authorised moderators (for chat and
reports) and to the following processors, within the limits of their mission:

- **Render Services, Inc.** (hosting of the website, game server and database; data hosted in the
  {{legal.dataRegion}} region);
- **Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.** and its affiliates (online payment, fraud prevention);
- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (traffic routing, attack protection), when this service is used in front of the hosting;
- browser **push services** (Google, Mozilla, Apple, Microsoft) for the Web Push notifications you agreed to;
- **artificial intelligence services** used only by the game administration (production of presentation
  videos): no player data is sent to them.

## 4. Transfers outside the European Union

Some processors (Render, Stripe, Cloudflare) are based in the United States or may access data from there. These
transfers are covered by the European Commission's **standard contractual clauses** and, where the provider is
certified, by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. You can obtain a copy of these safeguards by writing to
{{legal.contactEmail}}.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

Red Line uses a single, **strictly necessary** cookie: the `rl_session` session cookie that keeps you logged in.
The browser's local storage keeps your interface preferences. No advertising or social network tracker is used.
Details: [cookie policy](page:legal:cookies).

## 6. Your rights

You have the rights of **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **portability** and
**objection** (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR), the right to **withdraw your consent** at any time (notifications), and the
right to set **instructions regarding your data after your death**.

**How to exercise them**: write to **{{legal.contactEmail}}** from your account's email address, stating your
username and the right you are exercising. You will receive an answer within one month (extendable by two months
for complex requests, with prior notice). If there is reasonable doubt about your identity, additional
information may be requested. Deleting your account erases your account data; data that the law requires us to
keep (accounting) is archived for the legal period.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the
**CNIL** (French data protection authority), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France —
www.cnil.fr — or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

## 7. Minors

Red Line is intended for people aged at least **15**. In France, a minor under 15 cannot consent alone to the
processing of their data for online services (Article 45 of the French Data Protection Act): registration
requires the **joint consent** of the minor and the holder of parental authority. If you find that a child under
15 has registered without such consent, write to {{legal.contactEmail}}: the account will be deleted.

## 8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords hashed with Argon2, protected session cookie (_HttpOnly_, _Secure_,
_SameSite_), strict content security policy, restricted and logged administrative access, database backups.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change. In case of a substantial change, the new version is presented to you at your next login.
The date and version appear at the top of the document.
